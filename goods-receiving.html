Furniture manufacturing workflow

Goods
Receiving
System

A practical internal tool developed to manage incoming goods, track deliveries, and maintain receiving records for a furniture manufacturing workflow.

It gives store and purchasing teams one clear place to receive materials against purchase orders, note quantity differences, follow pending deliveries, and retrieve past records.

Purpose
Daily receiving control
Users
Store, purchasing, supervisors
Scope
Lightweight operational tool

A clear record of what arrived.

This browser demo uses fictional suppliers, purchase orders, people, and receiving records. It has no login, database, or live business connection.
